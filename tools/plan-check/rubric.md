# Rubric: is this plan ready to post and build from?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grounded diagnosis | The plan's stated cause and the change it targets, read against every step, control, timing and output in the repro evidence, and against causes proposed in the issue or thread (Diagnosis and grounding in the evidence guide). | The stated cause accounts for what the repro evidence shows, including any control or condition that made the symptom appear or disappear, and no repro observation contradicts it. The change acts on that cause rather than only hiding the visible symptom. A cause taken from the issue or thread passes only if it also fits this package's own repro evidence. A code-level cause the repro did not directly show is acceptable when it is consistent with every observation and the plan says where it lives. A restated symptom ("the data gets lost somewhere") is not a cause. | required |
| Bounded scope | The plan's in-scope and out-of-scope statements, named files or areas, and every step of its approach, read against the issue's reported behavior (Scope). | Every change the plan commits to is needed to fix the reproduced behavior, or to test or document that fix. The plan does not bundle refactors, renames, migrations, dependency or API changes, new features, or a redesign the fix does not require. Items explicitly left out of scope or deferred to a separate issue do not count against it. Touching several files passes when each touch serves the one fix. | required |
| Executable approach | The plan's named files, functions or components, its approach steps and their order (Executability). | A contributor who knows the repository but not the author could start the change from the plan alone: it names where the change goes (file, function, module or setting) and what that code will do differently. Intents such as "investigate", "poke around", "improve handling" or "fix the logic", without a location and a described change, fail. Exact line numbers or finished code are not required. | required |
| Decisive test plan | The plan's test plan and any verification promised in the comment, read against the repro evidence's steps, inputs and observed outputs (Test plan). | The plan names at least one check whose result differs between the unfixed and the fixed code, and states the expected fixed result: the reproduction (or a named reproduced failing test) re-run with its expected outcome, or a new or changed test whose assertion encodes the reproduced behavior. "Run the test suite", "make sure nothing regresses", "verify it works", or an expected result with nothing observable, fail when they are the only checks. Extra regression checks are welcome but cannot replace the decisive one. | required |
| Thread and conventions | The plan comment, with the plan, read against explicit maintainer statements in the thread and the repo's stated contribution and AI-use policy (Comms). | (a) Where an owner, member, collaborator or maintainer has stated direction relevant to this work (an approach wanted or ruled out, where the fix belongs, whether a change is wanted, who is already doing it), the comment follows it or names it and explains the difference so the maintainer can decide. Claims from non-maintainer commenters are not direction. (b) Where the stated policy requires something of comments or of the planned work (AI-use disclosure, human-written comments, a required step before a change), the comment meets it in the stated form. Treat course packages as AI-assisted work: silence about AI use does not meet a disclosure requirement. With no maintainer direction and no applicable policy ask, this check passes. Bug-report template fields are asks for the original report, not for a plan comment. This check grades what the comment does about direction and policy, not how precisely the plan paraphrases or attributes thread remarks; a loose or misattributed summary that does not change whether the direction is followed belongs to Calibrated claims. | required |
| Calibrated claims | The plan's risks, unknowns and Deviations section, and the certainty and promises in the comment, read against what the repro evidence actually showed (Honesty). | Unknowns the evidence leaves open are stated as unknowns or hypotheses, statements about the issue and thread are attributed to the right people, the comment promises no outcome or timeline the plan cannot back, and any recorded deviation says what changed and why. | preferred |

## Verdict rule

Accept only if all five required checks pass. Any required check graded fail or unclear rejects the package. Unclear means the evidence the check needs is absent after reading the locations the evidence guide names; it is never a softer pass. If the plan omits something a required check asks the plan to state, that is a fail, not unclear. Calibrated claims is preferred: grade and report it, but it never changes the verdict. A plan drafted before building may leave Deviations empty or say it will be filled after the build; that affects no check.
