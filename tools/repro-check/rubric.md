# Rubric: is this reproduction package ready to post?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific claim | Claim comment read against the issue's initiating action, conditions and symptom; Comms in the evidence guide. | The claim identifies this issue's concrete behavior and a relevant investigation or reporting next step. It does not substitute generic enthusiasm, blame or an unsupported assertion of completed work for an investigation. | required |
| Repo conventions | Repo-facts contribution policy and report-template asks against candidate comments; live policy locations in Comms. | The comments satisfy applicable explicit contribution policies, including any AI-use ban or required disclosure in its stated location and form. Treat course eval candidates as AI-assisted work; silence in a candidate is not evidence of no AI use. If policy requires all AI use disclosed with tool and extent, those details must appear. Policy silence or permission-with-responsibility does not itself require disclosure. For follow-up comments on an existing issue, report-template asks are evidence prompts, not a demand to repeat the original report: accept equivalent trigger-relevant information without redundant diagnostic dumps or duplicate-search boilerplate unless policy explicitly requires them in every comment. In claim-only mode defer asks needing a repro report. | required |
| Actionable environment | Repro environment record and commands against the issue's target platform, release and configuration; Environment in the evidence guide. | A stranger can identify the tested OS/platform, relevant software version or source revision, and the dependencies/settings that affect this trigger. Relevant differences from the issue's environment are explicit. Do not require irrelevant versions or secrets; do not accept an unspecified version or configuration when it could explain a different result. | required |
| Rerunnable steps | Repro starting state, installation/setup, input or fixture, ordered actions and commands; Steps in the evidence guide. | A stranger can reach the tested state and invoke the trigger using the public package and named public prerequisites, without private files, unexplained edits, hidden configuration or missing decisive actions. Commands, UI actions or a minimal example are sufficient when they actually cover the path; a bare instruction to set up or reproduce is not. | required |
| Right behavior proved | Repro output excerpts, logs, screenshots or concrete before/after observations compared with the issue's action, conditions and visible symptom; Behavior shown in the evidence guide. | The evidence records what happened after exercising the issue's actual trigger and distinguishes the expected behavior from the observed result. A reproduced result must show the same failure, not merely a nearby symptom or the same error under a different initiating action. A cannot-reproduce result passes when a concrete attempt of that trigger shows the nonfailure under named conditions. A setup/import error before the trigger, a promise of later evidence, or an unsupported assertion is not proof of either outcome. | required |
| Bounded honest outcome | Claim and repro conclusions read against their own artifacts, tested conditions and issue/thread facts; Honesty in the evidence guide. | The outcome is stated and supported: reproduced, cannot reproduce under specified conditions, or explicitly limited observations. No invented execution, overclaimed platform coverage, unsupported causal certainty, or claiming a different failure proves this issue. Hypotheses and untested paths remain labelled; an evidenced cannot-reproduce is as acceptable as reproduction. | required |

## Verdict rule

Accept only if every applicable required check passes. Any required fail or unclear rejects; unclear means necessary evidence is absent after looking at the specified sources, not permission to guess. Preferred checks, if later added, do not change the verdict.

For a live claim-only draft, grade Specific claim and Repo conventions. Mark Actionable environment, Rerunnable steps, Right behavior proved and Bounded honest outcome unclear with evidence `not yet applicable: claim-only draft`, and exclude them from the verdict. Specific claim still prohibits unbacked claims of completed reproduction. For a full package or any eval bundle, grade all checks. In eval mode use only the frozen bundle, never personal voice rules or current GitHub evidence.
